FAQ

Common questions.

Where does my data live?
Your data lives in your infrastructure. The platform is self-hosted by default — Docker Compose, Kubernetes (Kustomize manifests included), or our managed hosting if you prefer. Prompts and responses never leave your VPC unless you explicitly route them through a third-party provider.
Which models and providers do you support?
100+ models across 9 providers out of the box: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Mistral, Cohere, Together AI, and self-hosted (vLLM, Ollama, TGI). Adding a custom provider is a YAML edit, not a code change.
What is the SRE agent?
An LLM-driven agent that subscribes to incident signals from your stack — SLA violations, budget breaches, provider unhealthiness, guardrail violations — diagnoses the situation against current platform state, and proposes remediation actions from a constrained allow-list (failover, rate-limit tightening, cache clear, guardrail tightening, etc). Every action is gated through a human approval queue in v1; risk-scored auto-execution lands in v2.
What happens at the end of the 30-day free trial?
Nothing breaks. We email you 7 days before the trial ends. If you don't pick a paid tier, the platform downgrades to read-only — your data, configs, and audit logs stay intact for 90 days. No credit card is collected upfront.
Can we run this fully air-gapped?
Yes — Enterprise tier supports fully air-gapped deployments with offline license validation. All container images are mirrored to your private registry; updates ship as signed bundles.
How does pricing work for the LLM tokens themselves?
We don't mark up tokens. You bring your own provider keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Bedrock, etc.) — model spend goes to them at their list price. Our platform fee covers the control plane (proxy, governance, audit, dashboards, support).
Is the source code available?
The platform is delivered as container images and Kustomize manifests. Source-code access is available on Enterprise tier under a custom commercial agreement (escrow + read access).
